Adam knew that feeling.

Sometimes, when someone touched Adam's things, he felt the hot thing too — and shouted "That's not fair!" before he could stop it.

"It just happens," said Adam. "Really fast."

Blaze's eyes went wide. "You'd help me? Even after I burned his favourite football ball?"

"Especially after," said Adam.

"Then I'll think for both of us," said Adam.
