# GTales marketing plan (launch: 2026-10-17)

## Who we sell to
- **Buyer:** Egyptian parents (mostly mums) of 4–8-year-olds in Cairo and Alexandria: New Cairo, Maadi, Zamalek, Sheikh Zayed, October, Heliopolis.
- They read English with their kids, already spend on activities and books, and live on Instagram, Facebook mum groups and WhatsApp.
- **Their pain:** "My child hits / lies / won't share / is scared / is being bullied / melts down, and I don't know what to do."
- **Our promise:** a picture book where *your* child is the hero of their exact problem, with a science-based parent guide. Try it free, then order the printed book.
- **Gift buyers:** grandparents, aunts, birthdays, Eid, new school year.

## Message
- **One line:** "Your child is the hero of their own story — and learns to handle big feelings."
- **Proof:**
  - Show the real free story with the child's name in it.
  - The parent guide is built on research (cite it lightly).
  - Show the printed book in real hands.
- **Captions:** Egyptian Arabic + English for posts and ads. The book itself stays in English.
- **Offer:** `LAUNCH40`, 40% off the first printed book. 749 → 449 EGP + 89 delivery. Cash on delivery.

## Channels, ranked by expected return

1. **WhatsApp + friends and family (day 1).**
   - Every team member sends the free story link to 20 parents personally, with a short voice note.
   - Ask each one to make a story for their child and send a screenshot.
   - These become our first reviews.
2. **Facebook mum groups.** Find the 10 biggest Egyptian parenting groups. Read each group's rules first: many ban ads but allow "I made something" posts or have promo days.
   - Post as a parent or founder story, not an ad: why we built it, plus a free link.
   - Answer every comment within an hour.
3. **Instagram + TikTok Reels**, 4 a week. Formats that work for this product:
   - Flip-through of a real printed book with a child's name.
   - Kid reaction: the child hears their own name in the story.
   - "3 signs your child's tantrums come from hunger, not naughtiness" (from the parent guides).
   - Before/after: the stuck moment → the Combo (hug tummy, 3 breaths, Power Word).
4. **Micro-influencer mums (5–50k followers), weeks 1–3.**
   - Send a free printed book to 10 of them, personalised for their child, in exchange for an honest reel or story.
   - Give each a personal code (e.g. `NOUR15`) to track sales.
5. **Nurseries, schools and child therapists, weeks 2–6.**
   - Offer a 20-minute "big feelings" story-time session, free.
   - Leave flyers with a QR code to the free story.
   - Child psychologists can hand the parent guide to families.
6. **Paid ads, after we have 3 real reviews.**
   - Meta ads for mothers 25–45 in Cairo/Giza/Alexandria, interests in parenting and children's books.
   - Start with 150–250 EGP/day for 7 days. Keep the best creative, cut the rest.

## Numbers we track (weekly, in CONTEXT.md worklog)
- Visitors → free stories made (target ≥ 25%).
- Free stories shared to WhatsApp (target ≥ 15%).
- Free story → printed order (target 3–5% in the first month).
- Cost per order on paid ads (must stay under ~150 EGP to make sense at 449 EGP).
- Refunds/complaints and delivery time.

## Rules
- Only real reviews, never invented ones. Ask permission before posting a child's photo or name, and blur faces unless parents say yes.
- Never post a child's full name + school + area together.
- Every promise in an ad must be true on the site that day.
