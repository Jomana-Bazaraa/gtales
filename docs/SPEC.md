## 1. Product

GTales ("Gamified Tales"): personalised, interactive storybooks for children aged 2–10 in which the child is the hero and each tale builds one "superpower" (a strength that answers a real struggle: anger, fear, bullying, lying, big feelings, friendship).

Two offers:

| | Free story | The Series |
|---|---|---|
| Role | A taste. The top of the funnel. | The product. Everything on the site sells this. |
| Personalisation | Text only: name, age, boy/girl, place, other child, strength, sidekick name, Power Word, need/ask | Made from scratch: child's face from a photo in every picture, loved ones with their faces, strengths, struggles, a memory |
| Art | Fixed, pre-made art per tale: a boy set and a girl set. Never generated by the app. | Created from the photo by our team, checked page by page |
| Format | 3 instant PDFs: story (16 story pages), colouring book, parent guide for that one struggle | 3 printed books (each with some colourable pages) + 1 combined parent guide |
| Price | Free, never capped or advertised as limited | List 749 EGP [config], default promo LAUNCH40 = 40% → 449 EGP, + delivery 89 EGP [config] = 538 EGP, pay on delivery |
| Production | Automatic: text filled by `fillStory`, optional one-call AI polish, seconds | Manual by the team, approved page by page on WhatsApp |

Positioning: Series-first. The free story is the fallback, not the headline.
Market: Egypt, English only at launch. Domain gtales.kids.

## 2. Global rules (apply to every screen, every PDF, every message)

1. **No "psychology".** The word never appears. "Expert-reviewed" is allowed only once a named expert has actually reviewed the content (open decision, §16). Until then: "A parent guide for this struggle."
2. **No hand-drawn claims.** Never "illustrator", "by hand", "drawn". Use: "We create the illustrations from your photo, and our team checks every page."
3. **Never cap or advertise free stories.** No "one free story", no "unlimited". Say "a free story". Backend rate limit [config, default 10 stories / hour / device] is silent; on hit, show "Please try again in a little while."
4. **Positive framing.** Tales are named by the strength (§1.1); the problem is the subtitle.
5. **Images match words.** Alt text describes what the adjacent copy says. Missing art → neutral block "IMAGE NEEDED: <description>", never a mismatched picture.
6. **No fake content.** Reviews section hidden until 3 real reviews with written permission. No placeholder names, cities or quotes on the live site. Sample data in designs is labelled.
7. **Shorter page.** Each section answers the parent's next question once. Nothing repeats.
8. **Buttons:** short, encouraging, no child names, no essays underneath. Primary CTA everywhere: "Create your child's books". Secondary: "Try a free story".
9. **Words:** "approval", not "proof". "Our team", not "AI". "Tale" for a story in the Series, "story" for the free one. "Superpower" for the strength.
10. **Mobile first.** Every page is designed at 390 px and must pass §14.4 before desktop is considered done.

## 7. Free story: form and personalization engine (`/create`, 8 steps)

**Principle (decided 10 Oct):** the free story is personalised in the **text only**. Each tale has fixed, pre-made art (a boy set and a girl set); the app never generates or regenerates images. Personal details are filled into the tale's text by a pure function, with an optional one-call AI polish that may only smooth sentences. The Series is produced manually by the GTales team. The old Keeper/Blaze template engine and the image pipeline are internal tools only and never serve a parent-facing story.

### 7.1 Story files (one JSON per tale, `stories/<slug>.json`)

Source: the team's six story texts (anger, worry/anxiety, bullied, lying, feelings, friendship), 15 pages each, with personal parts in parentheses. Converted once to JSON without changing the wording. Placeholders become tokens:

| In the story | Token |
|---|---|
| (Name) | `{{name}}` |
| (Age) | `{{age}}` (words: "six", "six and a half") |
| (Place) | `{{place}}` |
| (Other) | `{{other}}` |
| (Sidekick) | `{{sidekick}}` |
| (Power Word) | `{{power_word}}` |
| (Need) | `{{need}}` |
| (Strength) | `{{strength}}` |
| (Snack), (Silly word) | `{{snack}}`, `{{silly}}`: only in the fun lines of `stories/variants/<slug>.json` (§7.1a) |
| he / him / his / He / His (hero only) | `{{he}} {{him}} {{his}} {{He}} {{His}}` |

`{{other}}` never gets a pronoun (we don't ask whether the other child is a boy or a girl).

Schema (zod in `src/stories/schema.ts`):
```json
{
  "slug": "worry", "title": "{{name}} and the Big Shadow",
  "uses": ["name","age","place","other","sidekick","power_word","need","strength"],
  "other_rule": null | "not_sibling",
  "pages": [{ "n": 1, "image": "p01.jpg", "text": ["..."], "sign": null | "italic text drawn on the picture",
              "challenge": null | { "type": "choice|riddle|steps|boxes|say", "question": "...", "riddle": ["..."],
                                    "options": [{"label":"Big","correct":true,"response":"Yes. Big cloud, big worry."}],
                                    "steps": ["..."], "count": 3 } }]
}
```
Challenge types: `choice` (tap an answer), `riddle` (choice plus riddle lines), `steps` (the 4-step Combo on page 12, each ticked), `boxes` (tick N boxes), `say` (say it out loud, no right answer). The bullying tale has `other_rule: "not_sibling"`.

Images: `public/stories/<slug>/<boy|girl>/cover.jpg, back.jpg, p01.jpg … p15.jpg`. Handoff README: pages 4:5 portrait 2480 × 3100 (the picture box is about 216 × 238 mm), covers 5:7 2480 × 3508. Decision (10 Oct): the delivered landscape page sets (about 2550 × 2040) are final; the template crops them with `object-fit: cover`. Extra page fields from the handoff: `screen` (+ `screen_after`) for the band's screen text, challenge `tag` and `done`, `**bold**` inside steps, story `back_blurb` and `struggle`.

**Design is the team's template, used unchanged (handoff rule 2):** `public/gtales-book/book.css`, `render.js` and the embedded Literata + Fredoka fonts. The app never restyles pages. `render.js` fills the tokens and builds cover + 15 pages + back cover; `fitPages()` sizes each picture so it ends where the text begins and warns on "too much text". The reference PDF to compare against is `docs/reference/worry-boy-template.pdf`.

AC: a check script confirms every `(…)` in the source became a token; every tale has both image sets; `uses` matches the tokens actually present.

### 7.1a Age variants and fun lines (`stories/variants/<slug>.json`)

Written by hand by the team, never by AI; the story JSON is never edited. `GTales.applyVariants(story, variants, form)` (`public/gtales-book/variants.js`, pure, returns a new story) runs before `fillStory`:
- `fun`: two short funny lines per tale, inserted after a given line of a given page only when the form has every value in `requires` (`snack`, `silly`).
- `little`: replacement challenges (two big emoji choices, no riddles) used when the child is 5 or younger; 6+ keep the originals.
The result is stored with the book, so the reader, print page and PDF all show the same text. AC: every tale × boy/girl × ages 4 and 8, with and without snack/silly, renders with no `{{`, no `undefined` (`npm test`) and no "too much text" from `fitPages` (`npm run check:fit`).

### 7.2 The form (one question per screen, auto-saved)

| Step | Asks | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which superpower should this story build? | picks the tale; pre-selected from `?tale=` |
| 2 | Hero's name · boy or girl · age | name 1–14 chars, letters, spaces, hyphens, first letter capitalised automatically; boy/girl picks the image set and pronouns; age 2–10 (written in words in the story) |
| 3 | A place they know: neighbourhood or city | free text 2–20 chars, e.g. "Maadi", "Smouha" |
| 4 | The other child: name + relation | brother, sister, cousin, friend; bullying tale only: a child at school or the park (`other_rule`) |
| 5 | What they're great at | noun that fits "Best ___ in Maadi": builder, artist, football player, swimmer, dancer, helper, reader, singer, or other (free text noun) |
| 6 | The child chooses: sidekick name · Power Word · favourite snack · silly word | cards from `stories/builder-options.json` (the team edits that file); sidekick also free text 1–12 chars (empty = Bolt); power word card or own text 2–24 chars; snack and silly word are optional cards (Skip) and only feed the fun lines |
| 7 | Need | the `need` cards in `stories/builder-options.json` (a hug, to talk, quiet time, to play); shown only when the tale uses `need` (step skipped otherwise). `ask` no longer exists. |
| 8 | Parent's first name · WhatsApp number or email | one contact required (no dedication: the template has no dedication page) |

Boy or girl picks the image set; a gender whose set is missing shows "Coming soon" in the form and is refused by the API, never substituted with the other gender's pictures (handoff rule). Safety: every free-text field is checked for rude or unsafe words in English and Franco-Arabic before it goes anywhere; on a hit the parent is asked to change it, and it is never printed. Footer on every step: "No photo, no address."

### 7.3 fillStory (no AI, always works)

`fillStory(story, form)` in `src/stories/fill.ts` is pure and returns the finished story JSON. It handles possessives ("Adam's"; names ending in s use one rule everywhere: "Wrecks'"), capitals at sentence start, pronouns by gender with the right case, age in words, and throws if any `{{…}}` is left or any used token is empty (never a book with a hole). Unit tests cover ≥ 10 forms including Jana, Yassin, Lulu, Abdelrahman and names ending in s, boy and girl versions of every tale.

### 7.4 Optional AI polish (one call per book)

After fillStory, one call to Claude or Gemini (`POLISH_PROVIDER=claude|gemini|off`, `POLISH_MODEL`), temperature 0.2–0.3, with the exact prompt in `src/stories/polish-prompt.md`. The reply is validated in code and the plain filled version is used if any check fails: same page count, challenges, options and correct answers; Combo steps word-for-word; name, sidekick, power word, place and other child still present where they were; no new names, places or characters; no page > 15% longer; no sentence > 14 words; valid JSON against the schema. Every rejected polish is logged with the diff. Result cached per story so a book is never polished twice. Only the filled story text is sent, never the parent's email, phone or photo. With `POLISH_PROVIDER=off` every book still works.

Submit → fillStory → polish (if on) → Ready screen in ≤ 10 s (p95). Rate limit per §2.3. Event `story_created` with time and `polished: true|false`.

**Ready screen `/story/[id]`.** Cover (the tale's cover for the chosen set), "Adam's story is ready", buttons: Open the story (sky), Story PDF, Colouring PDF, Parent guide; "Send to WhatsApp" (wa.me link with the story link, ref=share); "Make a story for another child" (ref=share). Series band as §6. No expert line.

## 8. Reader and PDFs

**Screen version `/story/[id]/read`.** The template HTML rendered by `render.js`, with `book.css` untouched, scaled to the phone width (CSS zoom), pages stacked vertically, `fitPages()` run after fonts and images load. Challenges are tappable: an option shows its `response` (green for correct, with a star and a short chime); `steps` and `boxes` tick one by one and show the `done` text when all are ticked. Top bar: close, title, PDF.
AC: no `{{token}}` or `undefined` in the output; no "too much text" warning; the girl book has she/her exactly where the boy book has he/his and nowhere else ("Where he goes" on page 6 is the Big Shadow and stays "he").

**Story PDF.** Same HTML rendered by headless Chrome: A4, `printBackground: true`, zero margins, `preferCSSPageSize: true`; wait for `document.fonts.ready`, call `fitPages`, print. 17 pages: cover, 15 story pages, back cover. Made on first request, then stored: the web server (Cloudflare Workers) can't run Chrome, so the request queues a `pdf_jobs` row and starts the GitHub Action `pdf.yml`, which renders `/story/[id]/print` (token-protected), uploads to the `pdfs` bucket and sets `books.pdf_url`. The parent sees "<Name>'s PDF is being made" (about two minutes) with a link to read meanwhile; the PDF opens by itself when ready, and instantly after that. Admin → Free stories shows each PDF's state with Generate / Regenerate. Pages 1, 8 and 12 must match `docs/reference/worry-boy-template.pdf`. Colouring PDF: only when a tale ships a line-art set (none yet; button hidden).
AC: no overflow, no empty tokens, no broken words; one boy book and one girl book per tale reviewed before launch.

## 10. Accounts, sign-in, My stories, order tracking

**Principle:** no passwords. Identity = WhatsApp number (the number we already use for approvals). Email magic link as fallback.

- A free story needs no account. The Ready link is the access token for that story.
- An account is created silently at the first Series order (or when a parent chooses "Save my stories" on free form step 8).
- **Sign in `/signin`:** enter number → 4-digit code by WhatsApp (valid 10 min, 5 attempts) → session cookie 90 days. "Use email instead" sends a magic link.
- **My stories `/my-stories`:**
  1. Saved hero profile card per child (photo while an order is open, name, age, place, favourites, loved ones) with Edit, **Order again** (pre-fills the order form) and **New free story**.
  2. Order tracker per order: Ordered → Being made → Your approval → Printing → Delivered, with date, what happens next, "Message us" (WhatsApp deep link) and "Edit address" (allowed until Printing).
  3. Free stories list: Open, Story PDF, Colouring, Guide.
- Status changes are pushed to the parent on WhatsApp (template per status) and visible on the tracker; the two never disagree (single `orders.status` field).

Order statuses (short list, one admin): `ordered` → `being_made` → `awaiting_approval` (sub-flag `edit_requested`) → `approved` → `printing` → `shipped` → `delivered_paid` | `cancelled`.

## 12. Data, privacy and retention

**12.1 Tables (Supabase/Postgres):** `books` (free stories), `orders`, `parents` (users: one row per email / WhatsApp number, id = hash), `pdf_jobs`, `profiles`, `heroes` (saved children), `events`, `feedback`, `promo_codes`, `settings`. Migrations `supabase/migrations/0001`–`0003`.

**12.3 Events never contain** child names, photos or phone numbers.
**12.4 Free-story PDFs** kept 12 months then deleted; the Ready link says so.
**12.5 Consent:** checkbox copy on photo steps: "I have permission to use these photos. They are used only to create the pictures and deleted when the books are delivered."

## 14. Non-functional requirements

14.1 **Performance:** landing LCP < 2.5 s on 4G; images AVIF/WebP with width descriptors; fonts preloaded (Fredoka, Nunito; Andika only on reader/PDF routes).
14.2 **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA; all chips are real buttons with `aria-pressed`; focus visible; colour contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for text (cta text is ink on #ff8a1f); reader supports 200 % zoom.
14.3 **Reliability:** generation retries once; failures logged with reason; admin alerted by WhatsApp on `free_story_failed` spike (> 5 in 10 min).
14.4 **Mobile checks (every page):** 390 × 844 viewport; no horizontal scroll; tap targets ≥ 44 px; sticky bottom CTA on forms; keyboard does not cover the active field; landing ≤ 5,200 px.
14.5 **Security:** admin behind sign-in + 2FA code; rate limits on sign-in codes (5/hour/number) and free stories (§2.3); signed photo URLs expire in 1 h; CSP; no secrets in client.
14.6 **Content safety:** generated text passes a banned-words filter and is re-checked by the team before any Series page is sent for approval.
14.7 **Internal studio:** the Gemini image pipeline and the old Keeper template engine stay internal (`STUDIO_ENABLED`), never serving a parent-facing story.
